About What the Probe Found

What the Probe Found explains space results from the data: Webb's galaxies, Voyager's last years, exoplanets, Mars. Measured vs inferred, papers named.

Who makes this

What the Probe Found is Vision Media's space channel on YouTube. The research and scripts are ours. The narration is a text-to-speech voice; we note that in every video description.

How we work

Each result we report comes from a paper, data release or mission page we name, and we label what was measured apart from what was inferred. Sources are listed in each video description and at the bottom of every article. Mission imagery is public domain, openly licensed or our own; the credits page lists it per video.
